Free QR Code Generator Online

Atechabad's QR generator creates downloadable PNG QR codes for website links, app links, plain text, Google Maps locations, Wi-Fi networks, audio links, PDF links and WhatsApp chats.

  • Use URL QR codes for websites, forms, portfolios and social profiles.
  • Use Wi-Fi QR codes to share network access without typing passwords.
  • Use WhatsApp QR codes to start chats with a phone number and message.

Barcode Generator for Products

The barcode generator supports Code 128 for general product or inventory codes, plus EAN-13 and UPC-A for numeric retail-style barcodes. Each barcode can be previewed and downloaded as PNG.

  • Code 128 supports letters and numbers.
  • EAN-13 requires 12 or 13 digits.
  • UPC-A requires 11 or 12 digits.

QR & Barcode FAQs

What QR code types does this tool support?

It supports URL, app store, text, map, Wi-Fi, audio, PDF and WhatsApp QR codes.

Can I download the generated image?

Yes. After preview generation, the tool shows a PNG download button for both QR codes and barcodes.

Which barcode format should I choose?

Use Code 128 for flexible internal codes, EAN-13 for 12 or 13 digit retail codes, and UPC-A for 11 or 12 digit UPC-style codes.
